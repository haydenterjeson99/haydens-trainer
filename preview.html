Palette notes

The four brand hues are used as fills only. Ink sits on top of every one of them at 4.70:1 or better, which is what makes them legal here. Against the ivory canvas the fills themselves run #34A853 2.90:1, #4285F4 3.38:1, #EA4335 3.72:1 and #FBBC05 1.62:1 — so every segment is directly labelled, separated by a 2px gap, and backed by a table view. That is the required relief, not decoration.

Text never wears a brand hue. It uses the stepped-down variants (#1E7E34, #8A6100, #C5221F, #1A66D0), each above 4.5:1 on ivory.

Light only, on every device. color-scheme: light is declared so the browser does not darken form controls, scrollbars or autofill underneath an ivory page when the OS is in dark mode. A dark theme, if it is ever wanted, needs its own re-stepped values rather than an inversion of these.

Colour-vision check on the intensity trio: worst adjacent pair is tempo–easy at ΔE 13.0 under protanopia and 25.4 in normal vision, both clear of the floors. Runs are ink rather than a fifth hue, and transit is a warm neutral, so the three intensity bands are the only place colour carries meaning.
